Share view

Creates a read-only link to a snapshot of your tracker. Anyone with the link can view your current courses and assignments but cannot edit them. Create a new link whenever you want them to see updates.
Note: for others to open the link, the tracker app itself must be shared or published first (use the Share menu in Perplexity, or ask me to publish it).
